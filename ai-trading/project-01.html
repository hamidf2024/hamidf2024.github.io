Project 1: AI Trading Agent — Reinforcement Learning

A head-to-head test of reinforcement-learning methods for S&P 500 (SPY) intraday trading on 5-minute bars. Every method uses the same data, costs, risk rules and out-of-sample months. The goal is the model with the highest net profit, then the highest win rate, with a reasonable number of trades.

What the results mean

  • PPO-based agents lead. Risk-aware PPO made the most (+0.61% / month) with fewer trades; plain PPO wins more often (45.2%).
  • Only 7 of 24 RL variants were profitable. Every non-RL baseline lost money, so the bar in early 2021 was low.
  • No edge is statistically proven yet. The project's bootstrap test can't tell PPO's result from chance (p = 0.39–0.77). Four test months is too few.
  • Training budgets were small and fixed for fairness, so these are floors, not each method's best.

Where we're ahead

More RL methods compared under one identical protocol than most platforms or papers, with realistic costs and cost-sensitivity checks.

Where we're behind

Only 4 test months and 2 seeds, small fixed training budgets, hand-written agents, and no paper trading yet.

Biggest lesson

Research shows seed luck and the cost model can reorder the winners. Longer test data and more seeds come first, before any profit claim.

Goals & objectives

1. Maximum profit

The top priority: the highest net profit after costs, on months the model was never trained on.

2. Higher win rate

Among models with similar profit, prefer the one that wins more often.

3. Reasonable trade count

Enough trades for the result to be trustworthy and practical, without overtrading.

Approach

One protocol for every method

DataSPY 5-minute bars, 2015–2021 (2008–2021 available)
Training72-month base, expanding one month per step
Out-of-sample testJan, Feb, Mar, Apr 2021
Costs$1 per side + 1 bp slippage, next-bar fills
Risk rules0.5% stop, 1% target, 2% daily loss cap, ≤20 trades/day, flat before close
Seeds2 per method (8 runs each)

Method families tested

  1. Classic RL: PPO, DQN, Double DQN, Dueling Double DQN.
  2. Distributional RL: C51, QR-DQN, IQN, Distributional DDQN.
  3. Risk-sensitive RL: CVaR versions at 3 confidence levels, Risk-aware PPO.
  4. Intraday-specialised: ET-DQN- and DeepScalper-inspired agents.
  5. Continuous control: DDPG, TD3, SAC, Distributional TD3.

All agents are written from scratch and gradient-checked. A leakage registry audits every feature for look-ahead bias.

Main challenges

  • Evidence Four test months can't prove an edge; more out-of-sample data is the first priority.
  • Compute A 3-minute cap per run forced 1-epoch training; several agents collapsed to "do nothing".
  • Data Intraday bars are a community source, cross-checked against known SPY returns.

Project 2 builds on this work: same data and protocol, adding zones, price action and regime.

Disclosures

All results are hypothetical backtests on historical data. They include modeled commission and slippage, but not every real-world effect (partial fills, outages, market impact). Past results don't predict future returns, and no edge here is statistically proven. Nothing on this page is investment advice.
