Where we're ahead

Transparent, explainable rules built from real annotated trades, plus a full trade ledger. Curve-fit results are actively rejected. Most commercial tools don't publish this.

Where we're behind

No trading costs in the backtest, 6.4 weeks of data, one stock and long trades only. Competitors test across years and many symbols.

What we're adopting

Costs, multi-year data, a basket of similar stocks, a relative-volume filter, zone-strength scoring, a short side, Monte Carlo, and a path to automated execution.

Goals & objectives

1. Maximum profit

The top priority: the highest net profit after costs, and it has to hold up on weeks the rules weren't tuned on.

2. Higher win rate

Among versions with similar profit, prefer the one that wins more often. The target is above 60%.

3. Reasonable trade count

About 1–3 trades a day. Enough to trust the statistics, without overtrading.

Strategy & parameters (V7.1)

ZonesDay's open, prior-day high/low/close, and intraday levels confirmed by a second pullback
EntryTouch an established zone and confirm on the same candle: a strong green bar or a long lower wick
RiskStop −0.10, floor +0.20 (−0.15 / +0.30 on wide days); stop to breakeven after a green close
ExitNext zone reached with no more strong signal; hard stop; end of day
NeverBuy the first print of a new low, trade after the cutoff, or use future candles

Research findings

  • My own annotated trades always exited at "next zone + no more strong signal", never at a fixed target. That became the default exit.
  • Re-trading one level many times a day was mostly losses. Capping entries at 2 per zone per day helped.
  • The opening-range zone works as an exit checkpoint, not as an entry trigger.
  • Wick "bounces" off the day's open that close below the open won only 44%. Rejecting them improved every metric (V7.1).
  • Rejected as curve-fitting: a wider wide-day stop that fit one trade's exact low, and an entry cutoff of exactly 14:35.

Data & methods

  • CNQ 5-minute bars, May 20 – Aug 14, 2026. Backtest range Jul 1 – Aug 14; Jul 1–24 kept out of sample for V7 tuning.
  • 11 annotated chart screenshots (14 real trades) used to align the rules.
  • No look-ahead: every decision uses only the candles and zones that existed at that moment.
  • One unified backtester reproduces every version from V1 to V7.1, with the same numbers on re-run.
  • Every rule change is logged with its hypothesis, result and accept/reject decision.

Main challenges

  • Costs Profit shrinks to +1.19 at 2¢/share and vanishes at about 3.6¢.
  • Small sample 6.4 weeks and one stock. Profit isn't proven across market conditions.
  • Regime Choppy weeks (Jul 6–17) stay below 60% win rate for every version.
  • Profit target +0.42/week gross vs. a 1.0/week goal.
  • Definitions "Strong" and "second pullback" still use provisional thresholds.

Lessons learned

My most common discretionary mistake was entering on the first touch of a new low before the zone was confirmed. The rules now forbid it.

A big jump in results from one parameter is a warning sign. Check trade by trade whether it's a real edge or one lucky candle.

With many small trades, costs decide profitability. A strategy that looks best before costs may not be best after them.

Real annotated trades are the best calibration source. They changed the exit rule more than any parameter sweep did.
