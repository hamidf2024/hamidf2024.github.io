Two things to know before relying on this ranking

  • Costs. Four of the five are same-day models that make about $0.20 per $1,000 trade. A cost of 1¢ per share is about $0.28 per trade, so they lose money once it is charged. C. Transformer probe, B. Field-Fusion GBM and D. Fusion gate are the only models that keep a profit after costs.
  • Break-even trades. The win rate leaves out trades that end within half a cent of the entry price. The same-day models have many of these: V7.1 percent-scaled wins 70% of decided trades but 46% of all trades. If a break-even trade is not counted as a win, the top five becomes Rule OR Model, C. How it is measured

Fair test

Models were trained and tuned on 2020 to 2023, then frozen and run once on 2024 to 2026. Nothing was adjusted after seeing those results.

Gross and after costs

The goals are gross, before costs. Robinhood and Schwab charge no commission on CNQ, but each trade still loses a little to the bid-ask spread. The page uses 1¢ per share for that.

Ranking rule

Overall progress is the average of three fractions: profit per trade out of 3%, win rate out of 75%, and share of months above 1%. The rule was fixed before the scores were computed.

Limits of these results

  • One stock. All results are for CNQ only, on one data feed (IEX), and are backtests, not real trades.
  • Few trades for the multi-day models. B. Field-Fusion GBM has 25 trades in almost three years, so its 0.99% average could easily be luck. Its range of likely values runs from about −0.9% to +2.9%.
  • A rising stock helps long trades. CNQ rose about 39% over the test period, and a randomly timed 5-day purchase made +0.35% on average.
  • 2026 has 11 months of data. Its months are judged on the same fractions (8/12, 10/12, 12/12).
