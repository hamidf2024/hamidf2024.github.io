Project 14: Property Scout

A daily search for ways to buy a home in Southern California beyond the standard listing: off-market, short sale, bank-owned, HUD, foreclosure, pre-foreclosure, for sale by owner, probate, auction and relocation sales. Every lead is checked with the seller, broker, trustee, court or agency before it counts. Working website name: SoCal Home Radar, "Discover more ways to buy a home".

Daily process

  1. Re-check: revisit every lead with an auction, hearing or bid deadline in the next 7 days.
  2. Scan: work through the sources for all 10 categories, newest first, in the six counties.
  3. Log: one record per address, with every category it fits as a tag.
  4. Verify: confirm with the listing broker, trustee, HUD portal or court calendar.
  5. Digest: summarize new leads, deadlines in the next 48 hours and status changes.

Success measures

  • The daily scan runs on at least 95% of days.
  • All 10 categories are covered every day.
  • Every published lead has a source, a last-checked date and a status.
  • No listing is shown after its auction, hearing or closing date.
  • At least 60% of published leads are verified within 72 hours.

California rules that shape the project

AB 851

Until Jan 1, 2027, certain unsolicited purchase offers are banned in specified Los Angeles and Ventura ZIP codes. No owner outreach on unadvertised homes there.

Civil Code §2924m

Eligible buyers can bid after some foreclosure auctions of 1–4 unit homes, so an auction result may not be final.

Probate confirmation

Court confirmation is not always required; it depends on the representative's authority. Recorded per listing.

Main challenges

  • Data Off-market ads withhold addresses, and foreclosure credit bids are often unpublished.
  • Timing Listings change daily; auctions get postponed and hearings move.
  • Records LA County recorder records can't be browsed online, and San Diego removed online parcel search.
  • Legal AB 851 limits some outreach in LA and Ventura until 2027.
  • Scope A sale label doesn't prove the home is still available.
