Texas rules that shape the project

First-Tuesday sales

Foreclosure and tax sales happen on the first Tuesday of the month, 10 a.m.–4 p.m.; notices are posted 21 days before. Next sale day: Dec 1, 2026. Property Code §51.002

Tax-sale redemption

Texas tax sales transfer a deed, but a homestead can be redeemed for 2 years, so a tax-sale buyer may not keep the home. Travis Tax Office

Seller's disclosure

Most resale sellers, including FSBO owners, must give a written Seller's Disclosure Notice before the contract. Property Code §5.008

How to apply for a HUD home

HUD homes are sold only through bids on the official HUD Home Store (hudhomestore.gov), which is free. You can't bid yourself: a HUD-registered real estate broker submits the bid for you. Applies to all three markets. Checked Nov 18, 2026.

  1. Find the home on the official site: Search hudhomestore.gov by state, county or ZIP. Each listing shows the case number, list price, listing period (Exclusive or Extended), bid deadline and whether FHA financing is available (IN = insured, IE = insured with repair escrow, UI = uninsured). HUD Home Store
  2. Get pre-approved or show proof of funds: Line up FHA, conventional or cash financing before you bid. FHA 203(k) loans can include repair costs. Some listings carry HUD's $100-down incentive for owner-occupants using FHA; ask an FHA lender that participates. HUD Home Store FAQ (PDF)
  3. Hire a HUD-registered broker: Only a broker registered with HUD (with a NAID number) can submit your bid. Most licensed agents can register; use HUD's broker search. Find a registered broker
  4. Bid in the right period: Owner-occupants (who will live in the home for at least 12 months) get an exclusive first period, usually up to 30 days for FHA-insurable homes, before investors may bid. Bids are opened at the deadline; HUD accepts the bid with the highest net return. 24 CFR §291.205(a), (d)
  5. Send the contract and earnest money: If HUD accepts your bid, your broker sends the signed sales contract and earnest money, usually within 2 business days. Earnest money is $500 for homes at $50,000 or less; above that the local HUD office sets $500–$2,000. It counts toward closing; you can lose it if you back out without a valid reason. 24 CFR §291.205(h)
  6. Inspect, then close: HUD sells as is. Inspect at contract acceptance and again before closing; closing usually takes 30–60 days. Check bid results and asset-manager contacts on the HUD Home Store. HUD bid results
