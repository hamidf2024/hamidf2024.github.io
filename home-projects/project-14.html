How it works

  1. Re-check: revisit every home with an auction, hearing or bid deadline in the next 7 days.
  2. Scan: work through each category's sources, newest first, in the six counties.
  3. Log: one record per address, with every category it fits as a tag.
  4. Verify: confirm with the listing broker, trustee, HUD portal or court calendar.
  5. Publish: post the day's homes with working links, lowest price first.

Before you apply or bid

  • Know the price type: an asking price, an opening bid and a probate minimum overbid are different amounts.
  • HUD bids go through a HUD-registered broker during the posted bid period.
  • Auctions and probate hearings usually need registration, a deposit and proof of funds in advance.
  • Verify any agent with the DRE license lookup.
  • Free advice: HUD-approved housing counselors.

California rules that shape the project

AB 851

Until Jan 1, 2027, unsolicited purchase offers are banned in 15 fire-affected Los Angeles and Ventura ZIP codes. Civil Code §2079.26

Civil Code §2924m

Eligible tenants and bidders can buy after some 1–4 unit foreclosure auctions, so an auction result may not be final. Read the law

Probate confirmation

Court confirmation and overbidding apply only to some estate sales, depending on the representative's authority. Courts self-help guide
