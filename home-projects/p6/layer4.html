At a glance
  • Goal: see what a subject property would realistically rent for long-term, so a standard-rental strategy can be weighed against Layer 2's Airbnb numbers and checked against Layer 1's own Net income/mo assumptions, rather than guessing at a rent figure.
  • Summary: 8 subject properties covered (5 California, 1 Texas, 2 Arizona/Phoenix), 120 live rental comps total across two spec tiers — 80 at the subject's own bedroom count, 40 one bedroom down — each sourced from Zillow, Apartments.com, or Redfin.
  • Inputs: none to start — pick a market tab, or type any address into "Check another subject property" to queue a new one for a full comp sweep.
  • Outputs: a ranked comps table per subject at both spec tiers, with $/sqft and gross-yield figures computed against the subject's own value — the basis for Layer 1's Net income/mo on non-STR properties.

How this was built

For the subject address, I searched Zillow, Apartments.com, and Redfin — three of the largest, most reputable long-term-rental platforms — for currently-listed rentals near 445 O St. Rio Linda's own 95673 zip has very little rental inventory (about 4 active listings at the time of this pull), so I widened the search to Zillow's "Rentals Near 95673," which pulls in the closest surrounding zips: Elverta (95626, directly north), North Sacramento/Hagginwood (95838, directly south), and Natomas (95835, farther south). Listings were prioritized by proximity first, then by how recently each was posted, as a proxy for how close it is to a live, real-world rent.

Two spec tiers were collected, per your instruction: 10 "similar spec" comps matching the subject's own 3 bedrooms (single-family houses and apartments both included), and 5 "lower spec" comps one bedroom down at 2 bedrooms — useful for sizing up what a smaller unit on the same lot, or a downsized rental strategy, would bring in.

A caveat on "recently rented." Unlike sold homes — where county records publish the actual closing price — there is no public database of what a long-term rental actually signed for. Zillow and Apartments.com only show current asking rent on active listings, not confirmed lease terms. This page uses "days since posted" as the best available substitute for recency: a listing posted 1–7 days ago is close to today's real market rent, while one sitting 40+ days may be stale or overpriced. The oldest two comps below (posted 42 and 77 days ago) are kept only because they're the closest geographically — flagged rather than hidden.
Full methodology & notes
  • Sources — Zillow ("Rentals Near 95673" search, exact-bedroom filter) as the primary source; Apartments.com and Redfin as two independent platforms to spot-check pricing.
  • Cross-verification — 5 of the 15 comps were independently found on a second platform with matching address and price: 5 Fire Leaf Ct, 7604 16th St, and 5772 Amnest Way on Apartments.com (7604 16th St even carries an Apartments.com "Total Monthly Price · Verified" badge); 1850 Club Center Dr #515 and 6820 7th Ave #11 on Redfin. That's a good sign the Zillow-sourced data reflects real, live market listings rather than stale or duplicate entries.
  • Similar-spec tier (10) — exact 3-bedroom filter, single-family houses and apartments both eligible, ranked by proximity to 445 O St then recency of posting.
  • Lower-spec tier (5) — exact 2-bedroom filter, same method. Two of the five are inside Rio Linda's own 95673 zip — the closest possible comps — flagged below.
  • Excluded listing types — building-level "starting from $X+" apartment-complex cards without a specific unit's price/sqft were skipped in favor of listings with a confirmed unit-level price and square footage, wherever enough of those existed.
  • $/sqft and yield figures — computed from each comp's own price and sqft, then simple-averaged; the gross rent yield compares annualized median rent to the subject's realtor.com RealEstimate ($365,000, from Layer 3) as a rough back-of-envelope figure only.
