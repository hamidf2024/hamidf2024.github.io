How this page works

For each address, this page scores the rental-management companies that actually serve that ZIP or city against a 9-factor weighted rubric — specialization, fee transparency, maintenance response, leasing performance, tenant screening, reporting/technology, reviews, licensing/complaint history, and contract flexibility. Every company's fee schedule, star rating, and licensing status is sourced directly from that company's own site, a review platform, the Better Business Bureau, or the CA Department of Real Estate's public license lookup — never estimated.

Built from a ChatGPT research thread Tony ran and shared for the 6316 Hillsdale Blvd property, cross-checked and substantially extended this session with independent BBB and CA DRE license lookups, plus an entirely independent research pass for 437 Forni Rd, which the shared thread didn't cover. A "check another property" box at the bottom queues the next one.

Read this before picking a company off the ranking alone: these scores are a starting shortlist, not a hiring decision. Fee percentages, review counts and BBB ratings all change; several companies here don't publish a management-fee percentage at all and need a direct call. Every honest research thread on this topic — including Tony's own — reaches the same conclusion: get a written rental-analysis proposal from at least two or three companies on this list before signing anything.
