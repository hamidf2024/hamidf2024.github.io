How this page works

Off-market sourcing has the same problem Layer 5 found for realtors: no single platform, list, or website covers it. A deep research pass across investor-education sites, paid data-platform comparisons, and each metro's own city/county government portals turned up a consistent set of categories every time, each with a real trade-off between cost, reliability, and how much effort it takes to turn a lead into a contactable owner.

So this page is organized as a sourced directory, not a leaderboard — tabbed by metro (Sacramento, CA; Phoenix, AZ; Austin, TX), each tagged 🟢 official public record, 🟡 paid data platform, or ⚪ community/relationship-based, with real cost, effort, and a metro-specific note on how to actually use it. A suggested order of operations, plus a step-by-step sequence for connecting a specific address to its lender/trustee/realtor, is below each metro's categories.

Read this before spending money or making contact: being on a distress list (tax-delinquent, code-violation, probate, absentee-owner) does not mean the owner wants to sell — it means they might. Every lead from every category here needs its own verification (confirm current ownership at the county assessor/appraisal district) and a respectful, compliant approach (see the Cold Calling and Bandit Signs notes in Sacramento's Field & Outreach category for the legal caveats that apply everywhere).
