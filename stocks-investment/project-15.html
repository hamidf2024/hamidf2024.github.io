Project 15: Gold Investment

A plan to add gold to the portfolio as a long-term diversifier, bought in six planned steps from December 2026 to September 2027 instead of all at once. Gold has fallen about 37% from its January 2026 record and is still volatile, so each purchase is sized and timed by written rules backed by 1970–2026 price research.

Investment thesis

Main idea

Gold earns its place over decades, not years. Central bank buying has raised the floor, but interest rates and the dollar still set the short-term direction, so the plan buys slowly and does not chase.

Four points from the research

  1. Decades, not years. About 9.0% a year from end-1970 to end-2025, or 4.9% after inflation. Annual swings average about 28 points, and a 1980 peak buyer waited 44 years to break even in real terms.
  2. Central banks raised the floor. They bought 675 tonnes in the first nine months of 2026, and a record 45% plan to add gold in the next 12 months.
  3. Rates and the dollar set direction. The 2026 fall came as markets priced Fed hikes, the 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5% and the dollar hit a 19-month high.
  4. Price below most fair-value estimates. About $3,470 today, against a stock-level model near $4,040 and a Goldman Sachs end-2027 target of $4,800. Both are estimates with wide error bands.

What would break the thesis

  • China China stops adding gold to its reserves.
  • Central banks Central banks as a group turn into net sellers.
  • Real yields Real yields keep rising for several years, as in 1981–85 and 2013–15.

Scope

In: physically backed gold ETFs, insured bars and coins, staged buying, reviews and rebalancing. Out: futures, options, margin, short-term trading, other metals, and mining stocks unless the owner opts in.

Roadmap

The first purchase waits for two things: the owner's approved brief (G1) and the Fed's Dec 8–9 decision. Buying then runs in six stages to September 2027, followed by long-term holding with yearly checkpoints to end-2029.

Rules that change the pace

SignalAction
Gold falls into the $3,000–3,400 areaBring the next tranche forward, at most one extra a month
Gold rises over 20% in one monthDelay the next tranche by a month
China reports no purchase, or a quarter of net central bank sellingPause and hold a review
Fed hikes again and real yields keep risingKeep to the calendar, do not speed up
Fed pauses or cuts and the dollar index falls below 100Keep to the calendar, do not chase

Hard limits: no single purchase above twice the planned tranche, and the total never above the target allocation. Each rule needs the owner's approval before acting. Once built, the position is rebalanced only outside a ±25% band around its target (for a 10% target, 7.5–12.5%).

Open decisions

Questions for the owner (Q1–Q5 due Dec 2)

  1. How much money in total, and what share of the portfolio?
  2. How long can the money stay invested?
  3. What is the largest fall you could hold through without selling?
  4. Which country and account: taxable, IRA or other?
  5. ETF, physical bars and coins, or a mix?
  6. Include gold mining stocks?
  7. Six tranches over about 10 months, or a different pace?
  8. Will a tax or financial advisor review the plan?

Proposed defaults

  • D1 Buy in six stages, Dec 2026 to Sep 2027
  • D2 No leverage, futures or options
  • D3 Wait for the Dec 8–9 Fed decision before the first purchase
  • D4 Monitor with three layers: direction, floor, amplifiers
  • D5 Treat forecasts as ranges, never as price targets
  • D6 Rebalance only outside a ±25% band
  • D7 Pause buying if China stops adding gold

Each default stays proposed until the owner approves or rejects it.
