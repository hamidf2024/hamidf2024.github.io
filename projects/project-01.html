Goals & objectives

Find leads early

Spot houses with a recent foreclosure filing before they are listed or sent to auction.

Verify before acting

Confirm every lead in county records, title, the MLS and comparable sales before setting a price.

Make sound offers

Submit a written offer to the owner or their agent only once title, payoffs, comps, repairs and funding support a price, with an agent and counsel involved.

Research findings

Sacramento County, CA

  • The search covers all of Sacramento County. Sacramento and Orangevale are reviewed first.
  • West Sacramento is excluded because it is in Yolo County.
  • The county recorder search returned "Request Rejected," so the filings are not yet verified.
  • There is no county application for a private purchase. Offers go to the owner or their agent in writing. California law gives some sellers in foreclosure a right to cancel (Civil Code §1695.4).

Austin, TX

  • The best owner-direct source is Travis County Clerk notices of trustee sale. The county appraisal district (TCAD) is used to match parcels.
  • Other sources are probate records and local agents and property managers. Tax-foreclosure auctions, HUD and HomeSteps are separate routes, not owner-direct ones.
  • Five candidates were excluded because they were already listed, at auction, or built before 1976.
  • Some Austin addresses are outside Travis County, so the county is checked first.

Limitations: the dashboard dropped the filing-date filter, county recorder access was rejected, and the provider's debt and equity figures are estimates.

Current methods

  1. Screen: apply filters for property type, value, equity, tenure, foreclosure stage and a build year of 1976 or later.
  2. Check each profile: open every match and keep only notices filed within the last 30 days.
  3. Cross-check: drop leads that are already listed, at auction or older than 1976.
  4. Verify: confirm the filing and any later documents with the county, then title.
  5. Price: gather comps, a repair estimate and seller-authorized payoffs.
  6. Offer: submit a written offer through an agent, reviewed by California or Texas counsel.

Next steps

  1. Verify each notice, and any later filing, with the Sacramento County Recorder and the Travis County Clerk.
  2. Have a local agent confirm the current MLS status of all 16 leads.
  3. Order title searches on the leads that pass, covering ownership, liens and whether a sale is scheduled.
  4. Gather comps and repair estimates, and set a maximum offer for each property.
  5. Engage an agent and counsel before contacting any owner in foreclosure.

Main challenges

  • Blocker The Sacramento recorder search is rejecting requests, so filings can't be verified online.
  • Data The provider's dates, debts and equity estimates are unverified, as the Ishi Cir lead shows.
  • Timing The findings are about 6 weeks old. Sales may since have been postponed, cancelled or completed.
  • Legal California's protections for sellers in foreclosure, including the right to cancel, call for counsel review.
  • Scope A filed notice does not mean the owner wants to sell.
