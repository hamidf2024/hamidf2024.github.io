How I test

Many trading tools publish only their best trades or a single backtest. Every trading project here follows the same standards instead.

1Profit after costs

Results are ranked by net profit after commissions, spread and slippage, then win rate, then trade count. Where costs aren't modeled yet, the page says so and shows the impact.

2Out-of-sample only

A model counts only if it holds on data it was never trained or tuned on. In-sample results are labeled as such.

3No look-ahead

Every decision uses only information available at that moment. This is checked with automated tests, not assumed.

4Beat simple baselines

Every model is compared with simple strategies and with published competitors under the same rules.

5Report everything

Full trade logs, all versions tried, and rejected ideas. Curve-fit results are called out, not hidden.

6Clear disclosures

Backtests are hypothetical. Every trading page says what is and isn't included.
